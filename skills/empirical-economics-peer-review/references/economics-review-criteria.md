# Applied economics peer-review criteria

Review the manuscript as an independent applied-economics or empirical-finance referee. Judge the argument against the evidence supplied, not against missing analyses that are merely convenient to request.

## Review dimensions

- Research question: clear unit, outcome, treatment, scope, and meaningful question.
- Literature positioning: accurate gap, relevant comparison, and incremental contribution.
- Theoretical logic: coherent mechanism, assumptions, and hypothesis derivation.
- Sample and data: transparent construction, coverage, timing, exclusions, and reproducibility.
- Measurement: definitions, validity, alternative measures, and likely measurement error.
- Identification: estimand, variation, endogeneity threats, treatment timing, fixed effects, and inference.
- Estimation: estimator fit, sample consistency, weights, fixed effects, standard errors, and clustering.
- Robustness: checks tied to assumptions, not a collection of unmotivated specifications.
- Mechanism and heterogeneity: testable channels, correct interaction/subsample comparisons, and interpretation.
- Economic significance: magnitude, units, support, and practical meaning beyond p-values.
- External validity: population, setting, period, and transportability of the claim.
- Writing logic: claim-to-evidence order, clear limitations, and consistency across tables and text.

Route detailed identification analysis to `economics-critical-thinking`. Route formal estimation questions to `empirical-finance-stata`; this reviewer does not replace either workflow.
